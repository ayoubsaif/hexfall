## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Coast rim vegetation
The dry rim between the coastal hexes and the shoreline rocks SHALL carry bushes and grass only, never trees (trees grow on forest hexes). The beach SHALL be narrow: its slope ends at shore distance 0.5.

#### Scenario: Coast without trees
- **WHEN** the coast dressing is laid out
- **THEN** it contains rocks, walls and bushes and no trees
