## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: 3D forests on Medium and High quality
On Medium and High quality, every forest hex SHALL carry 3 to 4 large, dense groves of tall conifers (only the faceted tall spruce and the 99.Miles pack pine), each grove 6 to 15 closely packed trees with the tallest in its middle and open ground between the groves, plus 2 to 4 boulders, one woodpile, one or two stumps, ferns, mushrooms, sometimes a small fallen log, and 18 to 26 grass tufts. Every tree SHALL be shorter than a city. Nothing SHALL overlap the number chip, the robber's spot, the hex's intersections (with a city margin) or paths (with a road margin); woodpiles, logs and stumps SHALL NOT overlap trees or boulders. The layout SHALL be deterministic per hex. On Low quality forests keep their painted art.

#### Scenario: High quality forest
- **WHEN** the graphics quality is High
- **THEN** each forest hex shows 3 to 4 large groves of tall conifers with clearings holding rocks, a woodpile, stumps and ferns
