## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: 3D forests on Medium and High quality
On Medium and High quality, every forest hex SHALL carry a dense mixed wood of 18 to 26 trees mixing faceted trees (spruces, birch, broadleaf) with the textured trees of the 99.Miles pack, 2 to 4 boulders, one woodpile, one or two stumps, ferns, mushrooms, sometimes a small fallen log, and 18 to 26 grass tufts. Every tree SHALL be shorter than a city. Nothing SHALL overlap the number chip, the robber's spot, the hex's intersections (with a city margin) or paths (with a road margin); woodpiles, logs and stumps SHALL NOT overlap trees or boulders. The layout SHALL be deterministic per hex. On Low quality forests keep their painted art.

#### Scenario: High quality forest
- **WHEN** the graphics quality is High
- **THEN** each forest hex shows a dense mixed wood with rocks, a woodpile, stumps and ferns
